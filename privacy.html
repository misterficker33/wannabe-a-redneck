Privacy Policy

1. YOUR PHOTO STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

All image processing happens entirely inside your browser. Your photo is never uploaded to any server. Closing or reloading the page discards it. There is no backend, no database, and no image storage of any kind.

2. NO TRACKING, NO COOKIES

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party services that profile you. The only data processed when loading the page is the technically necessary request handled by our hosting provider, which is not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

3. NO FACIAL RECOGNITION

The Redneck-O-Meter is pure comedy. It performs no facial recognition, no identity or demographic classification, and no biometric analysis. The score is a parody and not an analysis of anyone's identity.

4. CONTACT

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the address in our Impressum.

5. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we do not process your personal data on this site, there is generally nothing to exercise, but we will of course respond to any request.
